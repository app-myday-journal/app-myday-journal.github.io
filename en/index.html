How was your day?

My Day helps you record how you feel in a few seconds, look back on your week, and get gentle ideas for your days off.

  • Quick check-in

    Pick a mood, add a few keywords or a line of journal if you like.

  • Your data stays on your phone

    No account. Your journal is encrypted and kept on your phone.

  • Ideas for your days off

    Day-off plans and short advice when you feel low. AI suggestions are optional.

  • Help when you need it

    When things are really hard, the app shows emergency helpline numbers right away.

My Day is not a medical service and does not replace a professional. For people aged 16 and over.
