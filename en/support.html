Frequently asked questions

I forgot my app-lock PIN

On the lock screen tap “Forgot PIN?” and confirm with your phone’s screen lock (PIN, pattern, password or fingerprint) to set a new PIN. Your data stays as it is.

If the phone has no screen lock, the PIN cannot be reset. Set a screen lock on your phone before turning on the app lock.

Where is my data?

Only on your phone, encrypted. My Day has no account and we keep no copy. So if you change phones or remove the app, the data is lost and we cannot restore it.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete all data. The app deletes everything on the phone and asks our server to delete the anonymous install ID (if you ever used AI suggestions). Uninstalling the app also removes all data on the phone.

I don’t get reminder notifications
  • Check Settings → Reminders in the app.
  • Allow notifications for My Day in your phone’s settings.
  • Android: allow “Alarms & reminders” (on-time reminders) and turn off battery optimisation for My Day if your phone stops background apps.
  • Do Not Disturb / Focus modes can hold notifications back.
Does the AI read my journal?

Only if you turn on AI suggestions. Even then the journal is only used for a safety check and is not stored. See privacy policy, section 4.

When things are really hard

My Day is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number now. In the app, Settings → I need help now lists helpline numbers.
